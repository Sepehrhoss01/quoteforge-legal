About

What QuoteForge makes

QuoteForge produces short animated videos in which recreated historical figures speak lines they actually said or wrote — sourced from letters, speeches and published records. Each clip credits its source quote.

"Imagination is more important than knowledge." — Albert Einstein, as featured in a QuoteForge short

Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. This policy explains what QuoteForge collects when you interact with our app, pages or profiles.

What we collect

When you connect a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account to QuoteForge's publishing tools, we access only the account information and permissions needed to publish content on your behalf and to read basic profile details (name, handle, profile image). We do not collect payment information, private messages, or contacts.

How we use it

Account tokens are used solely to schedule and publish QuoteForge's own video content to the connected account, and to check that a post succeeded. We do not sell or share account data with third parties.

Storage & retention

Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as the connection is active. You can revoke access at any time from the relevant platform's app-permissions settings, which immediately stops QuoteForge from accessing that account.
